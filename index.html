01 • About Us

A foundation built around skills, healthcare and inclusion.

Established on 23 July 2015 and registered as a society, Pushpita Foundation for Healthcare focuses on healthcare and allied-sector skill development while also undertaking women empowerment and community-oriented initiatives.

🏥

Healthcare Focus

Training and support for healthcare-related roles, practical learning and healthcare-sector employability.

🎓

Skill Development

Industry-aligned learning combining curriculum, practical exposure, assessment, certification and career support.

🤝

Social Impact

Women empowerment, health awareness, community education, support for underserved groups and wider social development.

02 • Mission & Vision

Empower people with knowledge, skills and confidence.

The foundation's mission is to provide comprehensive and specialized training that strengthens knowledge, skills and competencies and contributes to a skilled workforce capable of meeting evolving industry needs.

Vision

To contribute to a diverse, inclusive and empowered workforce through skill development, economic opportunity, technological progress and community development.

03 • Programs

Programs designed for practical outcomes.

The documents describe healthcare skill development, women-centric empowerment, digital literacy, entrepreneurship and green-job training activities.

Healthcare Skill Development

Specialized learning in areas such as nursing, medical assistance, healthcare administration, medical coding & billing, pharmacy assistance and related roles.

Women-Centric Empowerment

Safety, health, digital literacy and entrepreneurial skill development for women, with a focus on underserved rural and semi-urban communities.

Green Jobs & Electronics

Training experience includes electronics, customer care and Solar PV Installation Helper under documented skill-development initiatives.

05 • Healthcare Services & Social Initiatives

Health Camps & Awareness

Health camps and awareness activities are described for underserved and remote communities, with emphasis on access, prevention and awareness.

Community Health Education

Programs cover health, hygiene, nutrition and disease-prevention awareness, particularly for communities with limited access to information.

Healthcare Support

The organizational objectives include supporting better treatment, care, welfare and rehabilitation and connecting patients with medical support networks.

Health & Sanitation

The documents describe potential collaboration on sanitation facilities, clean water and healthcare-centre initiatives.

Educational Support

Support for disadvantaged children may include educational materials, infrastructure development and resources that improve learning environments.

Women Empowerment

Skill development, education and gender-equality initiatives are used to strengthen women's socio-economic participation and independence.
